Powerlifting in Australia

A closer look at Australia's powerlifting scene...

Powerlifting is a competitive strength-based sport which focuses on three lifts: bench press, squat, and deadlift. Australia is home to a range of lifters, varying in gender, age, bodyweight, and experience, who compete in competitions from local to national championships. This visualisation will explore Australia's powerlifting scene, identify where competitions take place, investigate participation, and evaluate strength statistics.
